Shell and Soil Farms

A budding snail farm in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria

About Us

Welcome to Shell and Soil Farms. To be completely honest with you, we are passionate beginners and have not started full farm operations just yet. We are currently in the planning and learning phases—taking the necessary time to study best practices, prepare the right soil conditions, and set up a secure, natural environment for our future snails. We invite you to follow along as we build our farm from the ground up right here in Port Harcourt.

What We Plan to Offer

Once our farm infrastructure is fully established and our snails have matured, we look forward to providing the following to our community:

  • Healthy, organically raised table-sized snails.
  • Quality breeding stock for other new and aspiring snail farmers.
  • Cleaned snail shells for agricultural or crafting purposes.
