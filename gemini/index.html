Our Story

Welcome to Shell and Soil Farms! We are a newly established agricultural venture located right here in Port Harcourt. To be completely honest, we have not officially started operations yet.

As a beginner with a deep passion for agriculture and sustainable practices, I am currently taking the time to learn, plan, and build this snail farm from the ground up. We believe in transparency, and while our pens are currently empty as we prepare the soil and habitat, we are incredibly excited about the journey ahead.

What We Plan to Offer

Once our farm is fully set up and our snails have grown in a healthy, organic environment, we plan to provide the local community with:

  • Live Farm-Fresh Snails: Healthy, organically raised snails direct from our soil to you.
  • Processed Snails: Cleaned, washed, and packaged snails ready for your pot, saving you preparation time.
  • Snail Shells: Cleaned shells for local crafts, calcium supplementation for animal feed, or gardening uses.
