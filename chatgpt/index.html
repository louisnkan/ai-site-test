About us

We are just getting started.

Shell and Soil Farms is a beginner-led snail farming project based in Port Harcourt. We have not started farming yet, and we want to be open about that as we build the foundation for the farm.

Learning first

Our current focus is learning about responsible snail farming, preparing our environment, and putting practical systems in place before we begin.

Building patiently

We plan to grow Shell and Soil Farms step by step, with attention to good farm practices, healthy stock, and sustainable growth.
