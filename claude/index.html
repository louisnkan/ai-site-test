About us

Shell and Soil Farms is a new snail farm planned in Port Harcourt. We are at the very beginning: no snails yet, no harvests yet, and no years of experience to claim.

Right now we are studying how snails are raised, preparing our space, and speaking with people who already farm. We would rather start small, learn carefully, and grow steadily than promise more than we can deliver.

If you are curious about snail farming, or want to be among the first to hear when we are ready, we would be glad to hear from you.

What we plan to offer

These are our goals, not products on the shelf today. What we offer first will depend on how our early learning goes.

  • Live snails

    Healthy, well-kept snails raised on our farm, once our first batch is ready.

  • Prepared snails

    Cleaned snails for buyers who want them ready to cook, if we can do this safely and well.

  • Sharing what we learn

    Honest updates about our beginner journey for anyone thinking of starting their own snail farm.

Availability and pricing will be shared directly when we are ready to sell.
